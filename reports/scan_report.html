Contrarian Cash Scan

A ranking of US-listed companies against the checklist arden_trading uses in long-term TradingView theses on PEP, CMCSA, T, PYPL, SONY, F and INTC. The pattern: a hated, beaten-down company that still produces a lot of free cash, pays it out, trades below its peers, and has insiders or short sellers moving the right way.

    Where the top 150 sit

    Further right means more free cash per dollar of market value. Lower means further below the 52-week high. The author's picks tend to live in the lower right, past the Treasury line. Click a dot to open that company below.

    Check these by hand

    The scanner finds candidates. These parts of the author's process need a person reading filings and news.

    A catalyst

    Every pick had one: a spin-off (CMCSA, SONY), a new CEO (PYPL, INTC), an activist (Elliott at PEP), a finished restructuring (T), a product event (GTA VI for SONY). No catalyst means cheap can stay cheap.

    Sum of the parts

    The author valued SONY, F and T segment by segment (Ford Pro alone worth more than Ford). The scanner only sees the consolidated company.

    One-off cash

    FCF yields above ~25% (APTV, CMCSA, TDC, ADT) often include disposals or working-capital swings. The author warned that CMCSA's screener P/E of 4.6 was an illusion. Compare with company guidance.

    Captive lenders

    Automakers and equipment makers carry their finance arm's debt. For them the scanner scores leverage as neutral instead of penalising it.

    Macro

    Treasury yields against the dividend (PEP), the yen (SONY) and oil (energy) drove several calls. None of that is modelled.

    Structural decline

    The author asks whether a falling metric is a cycle or the new normal (CMCSA broadband losses, PEP snack volumes). Shrinking revenue is flagged in risks, but the call is yours.

    Run it again

    Both scripts sit next to requirements.txt in the project folder. A full scan takes about two minutes and needs no API keys.

    # one-time setup
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt

# full scan: universe, scoring, enrichment → output/arden_scan.csv + .json
.venv/bin/python arden_scanner.py

# rebuild this page from the latest scan
.venv/bin/python make_report.py

# options
.venv/bin/python arden_scanner.py --min-mcap 10e9      # large caps only
.venv/bin/python arden_scanner.py --enrich 200         # deep-check more names
.venv/bin/python arden_scanner.py --tickers PEP T VZ   # score specific names vs the universe

    Data: TradingView screener (fundamentals, estimates, technicals), Yahoo Finance (daily bars), OpenInsider (Form 4 purchases), FINRA (short interest).

    This is a screening tool built from public articles, not investment advice. The framework belongs to its author; the scoring and weights are this scanner's interpretation.
