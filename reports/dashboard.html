Watchlist by readiness

Readiness is the engine's estimate that the stock reaches its target before its stop within 40 trading days, shown as a percentile of every setup in its training history: 90 means a top-decile setup. Target and stop are sized to each stock's own volatility: about +10% and −6% for a typical value stock, more for a volatile one. The trigger level is the close that fires an entry signal. How the engine improves itself

  1. Every trading day it scores each name from its daily bars, records the call, and raises a signal when a name moves into a new stage.
  2. Every week it re-runs the full fundamental scan: new names that qualify join the watchlist, and names that miss three scans in a row leave it unless their move is under way.
  3. Every week it replays five years of history for about 300 names, labels every day by what happened next, refits the readiness weights, and re-tests every variant of the trigger rule.
  4. Changes need proof. A new rule or a new level of trust in the data replaces the old one only if it does better on the final year, which the fit never sees.
  5. It keeps score. Every call it made is checked against what the price actually did, so the track record above is out of sample.

Prices: Yahoo Finance daily bars. Fundamentals and thesis: the Contrarian Cash Scan (TradingView, OpenInsider, FINRA).
